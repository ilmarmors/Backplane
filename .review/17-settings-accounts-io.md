## Summary
The unit is one 187 KB file: the registry of named Claude and Codex logins (`profiles.json`, 0600, temp file then rename), each login's private store folder, the sign-in attempts (Codex's app-server device flow, `claude auth login` over pipes, `claude setup-token` on a pty for sync tokens), the status check (`verify_k`), the recheck before every turn (`launch_plan`), the 5-minute refresh, the usage reads, and the merge of sync accounts announced by linked machines. The parts that guard secrets are careful: ids are validated before they become paths, folders are checked for owner and symlinks, a sign-in link must be https on an exact list of hosts, provider credential variables are scrubbed from a named account's children, a caught token goes only to the vault, and every one-shot child has a time limit and kill-on-drop. Tests cover the happy paths, the #1108 hangs and the launch queue well, and the setup-token screen is a recording of the real CLI. The main risk is what a failed check does: the `status` op reads any failure of the CLI (a 12 s timeout, a missing binary) as "nobody is signed in", which disconnects a working account, lets the retire watch interrupt its running turns, erases a sync account's identity for good, and is never rechecked by the refresh; the desktop sends `status` for every account each time the account menu opens. Sync accounts have three more state bugs: a signed-out one reads `unchecked` after a restart and silences every announcement, Sign out and Renew on a receiving machine are undone within ten minutes, and the proof that a sync token works depends on a usage read its inference-only scope may not allow. Every check and every turn start rewrites and fsyncs the registry and moves the revision, which makes the hub append the whole inventory to the event log each time. Requests answer only after their CLI calls end, on the socket's own read loop. Smaller items: a test helper compiled into releases by a misplaced `#[cfg(test)]`, a dead `observe`, leaked candidate stores, and comments that no longer match the code.

## Missing tests
- A `status` whose CLI times out, is missing or prints no JSON, on a connected account with a turn running: the state, identity and catalog must stay, and the retire watch must cut nothing.
- The same failed check on a sync account, and with a locked token store: its email and plan must survive in memory, in `profiles.json` and in `sync_out`.
- A restart with a signed-out sync account next to a connected one: `sync_out` must still announce.
- `sync_merge` on a receiver that signed out, and on one that renewed locally: what the next announcement does.
- A usage `Get` that answers 403 for a sync token: a new token, and an expired account coming back.
- Two `claude auth status` answers that differ only by one missing field: the epoch must not move.
- A registry with an unknown `kind`, with an unknown key, and with mode 0644 owned by the user: what loads and what is said.
- `remove` and `sync_in` removal while a sign-in child runs and while `finish` is checking: no folder and no vault item left for the removed id.
- A hub restart during a sign-in: the candidate store is gone after `open`.
- Counting writes of `profiles.json` across `launch_plan`, `status` and `refresh` when nothing changed.
- Overlapping `refresh` calls: each profile checked once per round.
- `read_defaults` for Codex keeps the host's provider variables; `env_plan(.., false)` with a `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR` outside the accounts folder.
- `adopt`: a sync profile adopted without its token, the 40 limit, an entry with an empty label.
- `expire` during a sign-in (the attempt's `previous_state`), then a failed and a cancelled attempt.
- `respond` with control bytes; `parse_iso` with an offset; `valid_for` with a huge number.
- The Claude `auth login` path when the child's exit is never seen (the #1108 case at 1038 and 1047).
