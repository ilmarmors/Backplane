16. [25-kicad-embed] Answered (unit 53): a `detach` without `keep` for an evicted container does not free that KiCad. The hub sets `container = 0`, sends `AttachToParent{window:0}` and keeps the session `ready` (embed.rs:1516-1530). It is closed later only if nothing is unsaved: after 300 s unused (`idle_victims`; the detach itself resets the clock), at once when a new start needs room past three, or 15 s after the last desktop client that used KiCad disconnects. With unsaved edits it stays until the hub exits. A session detached with `keep: true` whose container is later destroyed without a word stays `parked` with the dead id; the fork's ET_DETACHED sets `lost_parent`, so the next attach re-parents even into an equal id, and being parked it is retired by the same idle and app-gone rules.
