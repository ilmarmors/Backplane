## Summary
The unit is the hub side of dictation: `session.rs` runs the `backplane-voice` helper per session, relays its NDJSON to the starting client as edits and views, accepts phone audio on `/voice/in`, and drives always-on and end-of-turn; `resident.rs` keeps one `serve` recognizer loaded; the rest are pure rules (draft placement, dictionary, settle, commands, end-of-turn, voice mark, ring notes and pages). The pure code is careful and well tested: positions are counted in Unicode scalars and all three clients apply edits in the same units against a shared fixture, the multipart parser bounds its parts, ring ids and paths are validated, the OpenAI key never reaches an argv. `/voice/in` sits behind the normal credential check plus a 128-bit per-session key and a 4 MB piece cap; the helper's stdin, stdout and `kill_on_drop` give clean orphan behaviour, and the resident socket is 0600. The main risks are in always-on: with the cloud engine (the default when no local model is downloaded) every voiced segment of the room goes to OpenAI although Settings tells the person audio stays on this machine; a helper that fails at once is restarted every second forever with its error hidden; and the "name first" routing drops the opening words of ordinary sentences and can send a message to an unrelated thread whose title shares a word. Smaller issues: an unguarded check-then-set race on starting a session, read-modify-write races on the voiceprint and on the end-of-turn learning counter, and a misleading contract about where messages go after a routed one.

## Missing tests
- Always-on with a helper stand-in that exits non-zero at once: the session must back off or stop, not respawn every second, and the error must stay visible.
- Always-on with the cloud engine: either refused, or the consent path is exercised (pins the privacy contract).
- `admit`/`route` with an unresolvable name ("No, keep it"): the words must be kept.
- `resolve` with sentence-opener words that appear in one thread title ("first", "now"): no route.
- Message after a routed one goes back to the open thread (or the documented behaviour, whichever is chosen).
- Two overlapping `start` calls (e.g. watcher and `read` restart): exactly one helper, `live` consistent afterwards.
- `/voice/in` with a valid credential but a key from an ended session (404) and with `end=1` before the stop (stdin closed once).
