## Summary
The unit folds the event log into `State` (mod.rs, fam.rs and the families beside them) and holds the policy core (`Policy`, policy.rs) that the hub's writer calls in `Transform::before_commit`; host.rs is the shell that boots it, checkpoints it and hands it to readers. The fold itself is careful: every reader of an event field has a default, I found no `unwrap`, index or `panic!` that an old, partial or foreign event can reach, and the hub and `Store::fold` contain a panic (the batch is written as it came, a line that breaks a fold is quarantined). Timer arithmetic is mostly saturating and all clocks are monotone by `max`. The main risk is that the running hub has only the lean copy, and the lean copy is not always the fold the tests check: it does not keep `todos.base.*`, so the three-way to-do merge never works in production. The second risk is the single `Mutex` that the writer shares with every reader: a panic in any view closure knocks the policies off for up to a minute, and readers that scan the task table hold up every commit. The recovery paths (poisoned lock, failed commit, boot window) lose derived events or apply them twice, and a read error during a tail fold is saved as a valid checkpoint. Auto-settle has one clear logic error (a queued hand-over holds a thread and its owners for good), and the fold of `ThreadArchived` was changed in place on 2026-11-15, against the project's rule. The stalled-thread half of the liveness policy is written and tested but not wired. Tests cover the pure laws well; there is no test of replay equality, of the checkpoint round trip, or of the lean copy against the full fold.
