## Summary
This unit is the support code around an agent turn: the parsers for Claude's stream-json and the Codex-shaped lines, the command ledger that decides when a turn ends, halt cards, design-history captures, wake subscriptions, hub-owned work (`work_run`, `work_watch`), delegated tasks, settle, and the tools that reach other machines. The pure parts (`lines.rs`, `cmds.rs`, `bg.rs`, `codex.rs`, `halt_view.rs`) are careful: a malformed or over-long line is counted and skipped before it reaches them, unknown types fall through, and they have law tests. The copy guard is applied consistently here (`hist.rs` now returns on a copy; the work and wake tools refuse). The risks are in the stateful parts. A watched job that passes its 20-minute reporting deadline becomes a row no code can cancel or re-arm. "Start fresh" on a halt card does not start a fresh session for Claude. Wakes and task results can be lost or wrongly reported across a restart because recovery and the two log followers do not agree on who tells what. Two tokio mutexes are held across a delivery into a thread's actor. Work output has no cap and is never removed. `halt_act` has no test at all, and no test restarts a hub with a timed-out job, a wake on a cut thread, or a delegated child waiting in the carry line. Three docs and several module headers describe behaviour the code does not have.
